## 3. Identifiers

- The type is `uuid`, version 7 or another time-ordered variant, **generated by
  the application** before the insert.
- Monotonicity is mandatory: a random UUID destroys insert locality in the index,
  and at these volumes that is measurable.
- No sequences, no identity columns, no database-side identifier generation, and
  therefore no trigger whose job is to fill a primary key.
- Natural keys (a currency code, an account number, a contract number) remain a
  separate column with a unique index but **never become the primary key**: they
  change more often than one expects, and their change breaks references in a
  cascade.
- Human-facing document numbers are allocated by `platform.document_number`,
  which is a domain concept with its own rules (per company, per year, gapless or
  not — the domain decides), not a side effect of storage.
- No table carries an identifier from another system as its own key. If such a
  value must be kept, it is a column named `external_id` together with
  `external_system`, with a unique index on the pair.
